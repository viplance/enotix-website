Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Enotix collects, why we use it, and the choices available to you when you use our website and services.

Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Information you provide, including messages or prompts you submit through our website and information you send when contacting us.
  • Basic technical and usage information, such as browser type, device type, IP address, pages viewed, and diagnostic data.
  • Cookies or similar technologies required to operate, secure, and understand the performance of our services.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide and maintain our website and services.
  • Respond to prompts, requests, and support inquiries.
  • Monitor reliability, prevent abuse, and improve performance.
  • Comply with applicable law and protect our legal rights.

AI prompts

Text entered into the “Ask Enotix” field is sent to our service for processing so we can return a response. Please do not include passwords, financial details, health information, or other sensitive personal information in a prompt.

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us host, secure, analyze, and operate our services. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or as part of a business transaction such as a merger or acquisition.

Data retention and security

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including security and legal obligations. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. You may also be able to object to certain processing or withdraw consent. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security purposes.

Children’s privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our services or legal obligations change. We will post the revised policy here and update the date at the top of the page.

Contact us

If you have a privacy question or want to exercise your rights, email us at privacy@e-notix.com.
